# Coin Screening Policy

## Purpose

CryptoLens provides an **educational research screen** for digital assets. It is not a fatwa, Shariah certification, individual legal opinion, or recommendation to buy, sell, or hold an asset.

## Screening questions

The interface asks whether an asset’s backing or economic claim is documented, what utility is publicly stated, whether interest-related activity is disclosed, whether the structure appears highly speculative, and whether project information is transparent. A missing answer remains **unknown**; it is never converted into a halal or haram conclusion.

## Interpretation

Islamic-finance sources commonly describe prohibitions relating to riba (interest), excessive uncertainty (gharar), and gambling (maysir), while emphasizing real economic activity, risk sharing, and avoidance of prohibited activities.[1] The platform uses these concepts only as prompts for further research. A qualified scholar or Shariah adviser should evaluate the relevant asset, transaction, jurisdiction, and user circumstances before anyone relies on a conclusion.
